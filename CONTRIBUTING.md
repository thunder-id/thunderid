Please refer to the [Contributing Guide](https://thunderid.dev/community/overview) for guidelines on how to contribute to this project.

Before you work on a bug or feature, comment on its issue, share it in `#thunderid-dev` on the [LF Decentralized Trust Discord](https://discord.lfdecentralizedtrust.org/), and wait for a maintainer to assign it to you. See [Get the Issue Assigned](https://thunderid.dev/community/contributing/contributing-code/pull-request-workflow#1-get-the-issue-assigned).
