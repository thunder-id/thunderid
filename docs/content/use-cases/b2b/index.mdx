# Serve Business Customers as Organizations

Serve companies instead of individuals from one shared deployment of your business-to-business (B2B)
software-as-a-service (SaaS) application. Each customer company signs up as its own organization, brings its people in
by invitation or through its own identity provider, and manages its own team.
