# Customize the Recovery Email

The recovery flow you built sends customers an email with a link to reset their password. Now you will make that email Wayfinder's own: change what it says, see it with Wayfinder's theme, and check how it reads in another language.

<Stepper stepNode="h2" as="h2">

## Edit the recovery email

In the Console, navigate to **Notification Templates → Email Templates** and edit **Password Recovery**. Change the subject and message, but keep the recovery link so customers can reset their passwords.

The editor shows placeholders for details that change from one email to the next. Use them where you need those details. For words that should appear in the customer's language, use translation keys and add the translated text in [Manage Translations](../../../guides/i18n/manage-translations). Save the email when you are done.

## Check the preview

In **Preview**, choose Wayfinder's theme and a language. You can see the message with the application's logo and colors before sending it. Switch to the language you added and check that the subject and message read correctly.

If you want to change the logo or colors, edit Wayfinder's theme in [Design](../../../guides/design/themes), then check the preview again.

## Send a recovery email

Follow the [Account Recovery walkthrough](../try-it-out/account-recovery/) and open the email the customer receives. Check that it contains your message, uses Wayfinder's theme, and appears in the customer's language.

</Stepper>
