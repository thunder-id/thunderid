# Identity for Personal Agents

Your customers bring their own personal agents, AI agents that act for them, to call your APIs. Each one needs an
identity tied to the customer who brought it, and access only that customer can approve. This section works
through the questions those agents raise, and how <ProductName /> answers each one.
